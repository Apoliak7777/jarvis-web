You pay for your AI subscription directly to the provider

Jarvis uses your ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini accounts – you pay them for the subscription, with no mark-up from me. Without a subscription it works with free AI services and the offline model; the answers are simpler then.

What if you stop paying

Your licence is valid until the last day you have paid for, then you have 14 days to renew before Jarvis locks. Your data stays on your computer and you can export it at any time.
