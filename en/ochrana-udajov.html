Privacy policy

I process personal data only to the extent I need to handle your order, licence and support, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Act No. 18/2018 Z. z. (the Slovak Personal Data Protection Act).

Controller

What data and what for

  • Order and contract – name, e-mail, phone, address, and for a company the business name, company ID (IČO), tax ID (DIČ) and VAT ID (IČ DPH), plus the content of the order. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Licence – the licence key contains your name or company name, e-mail, company ID (IČO), licence type, number of computers and expiry date. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
  • Payments and documents – I keep payment data and documents in accordance with tax and accounting regulations. Legal basis: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Downloads and updates – when you download the installer and when Jarvis checks for a new version (about every 6 hours), the server stiahnut.apoliak.online records the IP address, the time and the downloaded file. Jarvis sends none of your data and not the licence key. The records serve security and troubleshooting and delete themselves after 14 days. Legal basis: legitimate interest in secure operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Support and communication – e-mails and support notes. Legal basis: performance of a contract and legitimate interest in handling your request and defending legal claims (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Jarvis server – when you turn on Online bookings, Quote with an Accept button, AI assistant for your website or Team, your Jarvis signs in to the server stiahnut.apoliak.online with its licence key. The server records the data from the licence (number, company name, extensions and validity), the computer’s name and the time of the last connection. Legal basis: performance of a contract.

Your customers’ data on the Jarvis server

Bookings, quotes with an Accept button, the website assistant and Team need a server that runs even when your computer is off. It is in a data centre in the EU (Frankfurt) and holds only the data these functions need:

  • Online bookings – your services and free slots (not the content of your calendar) and the bookings: service, appointment, and the customer’s name, e-mail, phone and note. A booking is deleted 60 days after the appointment.
  • Quote with an Accept button – the quote and its PDF, and the name and note given on acceptance. It is deleted 30 days after it expires or is accepted.
  • AI assistant for your website – a visitor’s question passes through the server to your Jarvis and the answer comes back – the server keeps the answer for 10 minutes at most. The contact form (name, e-mail, phone, message) waits on the server until your Jarvis takes it over.
  • Team – changes to Clients, tasks and team notes are encrypted with a key that only your team has. The server cannot read them.

Whatever your Jarvis takes over (a new booking, an accepted quote, a question, a contact) is deleted by the server right after it is taken over, at the latest after 30 days. The server does not store visitors’ IP addresses – it keeps them in memory for 24 hours at most to limit abuse (the number of bookings and questions from one address). When your licence ends, your company’s data are deleted from the server no later than 74 days after it ends.

For these data you are the controller – they are your customers and the visitors to your website – and I am your processor under Art. 28 GDPR. The terms of the processing are in the terms and conditions (Art. 12). You set the privacy text for your customers in Jarvis, both for bookings and for the assistant.

Data in your Jarvis

Jarvis runs on your computer. Your mail, clients, notes, chat history and passwords are stored there, and I have no access to them. The text you write to Jarvis is sent by Jarvis only to the AI service you choose (e.g. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) – under that service’s terms; with an offline model it goes nowhere. During remote installation or help I can see your screen only for the duration of a connection that you allow yourself and can end at any time, and I do not store anything from it.

This website

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Your basket and your orders are stored only in your browser (localStorage) until you send the order yourself by e-mail. The licence key on the My licence page is checked directly in your browser and is not sent anywhere.

Who I share data with

I do not sell data or provide it for marketing purposes. Only the providers through whom I run the service may have access to it: the e-mail provider, the provider of the server for downloads, updates and the Jarvis server, the bank (for payments) and possibly an accountant. I do not transfer data to countries outside the EU, except where the service you have chosen requires it.

How long I keep it

I keep contract and licence data for the duration of the contract and then for no more than 4 years for possible claims; payment documents for as long as tax and accounting regulations require (at most 10 years).

Your rights

  • access to your data, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing,
  • data portability and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest,
  • a complaint to the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic, Hraničná 12, 820 07 Bratislava (www.dataprotection.gov.sk).

Just write to apoliak@apoliak.online and I will reply within a month at the latest.
