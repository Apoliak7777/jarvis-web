Jarvis tailored to your company

Every plan includes the whole of Jarvis. Companies add extensions for their own work on top – invoices, documents for the accountant, company knowledge, bookings or team collaboration – plus a setup for their field.

Monthly, on top of your plan

You pay for an extension together with your plan, monthly or yearly. When you no longer need it, you simply don’t renew it for the next period.

Switched on by a new key

A new licence key, which I send you by e-mail, unlocks the extension. Nothing is reinstalled and your data stays.

Built on what Jarvis already does

Extensions use the mail, clients, voice and documents you already have in Jarvis – you have nothing new to learn.
