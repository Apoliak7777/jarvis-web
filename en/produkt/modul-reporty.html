Business extensions

Owner overview

Every Monday morning you get a summary of your company: revenue and expenses compared with last week and last month, new leads, quotes in play, what is waiting to be paid and which tasks are overdue. At the end of the month a closing report as a PDF, which you can send to your partner or your accountant. Plus When the money runs out: the account balance in 30, 60 and 90 days by the due dates of invoices, documents and regular payments, and a warning if it would drop below zero.

Free in every plan: the This week card in Business with sales, unpaid invoices and new leads.
