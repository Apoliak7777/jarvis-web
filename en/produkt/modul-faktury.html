Business extensions

Invoices and reminders

You issue an invoice with a payment QR code straight from the customer’s card – with numbering, as a PDF and, at the same time, as a Peppol e-invoice for the mandatory e-invoicing from 2027. Send it by e-mail in one click; it is recorded in Business and Jarvis watches the due date: 3 and 14 days after it, it prepares a reminder that you simply check and send. Payments from the bank: a statement from internet banking (XML or CSV, with Fio banka automatically) is matched to invoices and documents by the variable symbol and the amount. It works for VAT-registered and non-registered businesses alike.

Free in every plan: a payment request with a PAY by square QR code and a text for the customer.
